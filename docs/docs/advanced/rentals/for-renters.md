# Rentals: Guide for Renters

The Rental Marketplace allows you to access unique experiences without the commitment of a full purchase.

### 1. Finding and Renting an Asset

1.  Navigate to the **"/rentals"** page to browse all assets currently available for rent.
2.  Each card displays the asset, the price, and the valid rental period.
3.  Click the **"Rent Now"** button on any item you wish to rent.
4.  You will be prompted to sign a transaction to pay the rental price.

### 2. Your Rental Receipt

Upon successful payment, you will receive a **`RentalReceipt` NFT** in your wallet. This is a non-transferable "ticket" that proves your right to use the experience.

You can view your active rentals in your **Dashboard** under the **"My Rentals"** tab. This receipt is what you would present to a provider to be redeemed (e.g., by having them scan its QR code). Once the rental period ends, the receipt expires and has no further utility.
