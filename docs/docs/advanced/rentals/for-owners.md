# Rentals: Guide for Owners

The Rental Marketplace is a powerful tool to earn yield on your `ExperienceNFTs` and `Fractions`.

### 1. Listing an Asset for Rent

From your **Dashboard**, in the "My Collection" or "My Inventory" tab, every asset you own has an "Actions" menu.
1.  Click the menu and select **"List for Rent"**.
2.  A modal will appear where you must define the rental terms:
    * **Currency:** SUI or TKT.
    * **Price:** The total price for the entire rental period.
    * **Start & End Date:** The duration for which the asset will be usable by the renter.
3.  Upon submitting the transaction, your asset is transferred to the secure `RentalListing` escrow contract.

### 2. Managing Your Rental Listings

In your Dashboard, under the **"My Rentals"** tab, you will find a section for "Items I've Listed for Rent". Here you can:
* **Delist:** If your item has not yet been rented by anyone, you can cancel the listing to immediately reclaim your asset.
* **Reclaim:** Once the rental period (`end_timestamp_ms`) has passed, a "Reclaim" button will appear. Clicking this will return your original `Fraction` or `ExperienceNFT` from the escrow back to your wallet.
