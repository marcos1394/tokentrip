# Evolving NFTs: Guide for Collectors

The Evolving NFTs you collect are living digital assets. Their story unfolds over time based on the rules set by their original creator.

### Viewing Evolutions

On the detail page of any Evolving NFT, you will find a dedicated "Evolutions" section. This area shows:
* **Completed Evolutions:** A history of the changes the NFT has already undergone.
* **Pending Evolutions:** A preview of what the NFT can become in the future and the conditions required for the change.

### Triggering an Evolution

The evolution of an NFT does not happen automatically. It must be initiated by a transaction on the Sui network.

If you are viewing an NFT and notice that one of its evolution conditions has been met (e.g., the trigger date has passed), an **"Evolve Now"** button will appear.

**Anyone**—not just the owner—can click this button and sign the transaction to trigger the evolution. This is a community-driven action that helps keep the state of all assets on TokenTrip up-to-date. Once the transaction is confirmed, the NFT's metadata will be permanently updated for everyone to see.
