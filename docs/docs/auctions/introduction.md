# Introduction to the TokenTrip Auction House

Welcome to the TokenTrip Auction House, a fully on-chain, decentralized, and fair marketplace for unique experiences. Our auction system is designed to provide a professional and secure environment for both sellers and bidders.

### Key Features

Our smart contract enables a robust auction experience with several key features:

* **Multi-Currency Support:** Sellers can choose to run auctions in either **SUI** or our native **TKT** token, providing flexibility and utility.
* **Reserve Price:** Sellers can set a secret minimum price. If the auction doesn't meet the reserve, the NFT is safely returned to the seller, protecting them from underselling valuable assets.
* **Anti-Sniping:** To ensure fair price discovery, any bid placed in the last 5 minutes of an auction will automatically extend the end time, giving other bidders a chance to respond.
* **Creator Royalties:** The original creator of an `ExperienceNFT` automatically receives a royalty percentage on every successful auction sale on the secondary market.
* **Decentralized Settlement:** Once an auction ends, **anyone** can trigger the settlement function to finalize the transfer of the NFT and funds, ensuring the marketplace runs autonomously without relying on any central party.
