# Leaving a Review

Your feedback is a vital part of the TokenTrip ecosystem. By leaving reviews, you help other travelers make informed decisions and reward good providers.

### Using Your Purchase Receipt

Only users who have verifiably purchased an experience can leave a review. Your authorization to do so is the **Purchase Receipt NFT** you receive after a successful purchase.

1.  **Go to Your Portfolio**: Navigate to the "My Collection" or a similar portfolio section on the landing page after connecting your wallet.
2.  **Find Your Receipt**: Under the "Pending Reviews" tab, you will see cards for experiences you've purchased but haven't yet reviewed.
3.  **Click "Leave a Review"**: This will take you to the review page, pre-filled with the details of the experience you're rating.
4.  **Submit Your Rating**: Choose a star rating (1-5) and write a helpful comment about your experience.
5.  **Publish On-Chain**: Click "Publish Review" and approve the transaction. Your review will be permanently recorded on the Sui blockchain, and the Purchase Receipt NFT will be consumed to prevent duplicate reviews.
