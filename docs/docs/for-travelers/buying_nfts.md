# How to Buy an Experience

Purchasing on TokenTrip is a transparent and secure on-chain process.

### Step-by-Step Guide

1.  **Find an Experience**: Navigate to the detail page of the experience you wish to purchase.
2.  **Check the Details**: Review the description, price, and seller information. The page will clearly indicate if the sale is a primary sale from the **Original Creator** or a secondary market **Resale** from another user.
3.  **Click "Buy Now"**: This will prompt your wallet (either your zkLogin session or your connected browser wallet) to approve the transaction.
4.  **Confirm the Transaction**: The wallet pop-up will show you the assets being exchanged—the SUI or TKT you are spending and the Experience NFT you are receiving. Approve the transaction.
5.  **Success!**: Once the transaction is confirmed on the Sui network (usually within seconds), the Experience NFT is transferred directly to your wallet. You will also receive a **Purchase Receipt NFT**, which serves as your proof of purchase and your ticket to leave a review later.
