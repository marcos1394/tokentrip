# Browse the Marketplace

The TokenTrip marketplace is your gateway to discovering unique experiences around the world.

### Explore Page
The main **Explore** page features a grid of all currently available experiences listed for a fixed price. You can browse, see the price in SUI or TKT, and click on any card to view its detailed page.

### Auctions
The **Auctions** page showcases special or rare experiences available for bidding. Here you can see the current highest bid and the time remaining for each auction. To participate, simply click on an auction to go to its detail page and place your bid.
