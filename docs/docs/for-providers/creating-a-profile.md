# Becoming a Provider

Ready to tokenize your unique experiences and reach a global audience? The first step is to create your on-chain Provider Profile.

1.  **Navigate to "Become a Provider"**: You'll find a link on our homepage or in the site's main navigation.
2.  **Connect Your Wallet**: Ensure your Sui wallet is connected. This wallet will be the owner of your provider profile and will receive all payments.
3.  **Fill Out Your Profile**:
    * **Store or Brand Name**: The public name for your storefront.
    * **Short Bio**: A brief description of the types of experiences you offer.
    * **Logo or Profile Image URL**: A direct link to an image that represents your brand.
4.  **Submit the Transaction**: Click "Create Provider Profile" and approve the transaction in your wallet.

Once the transaction is confirmed, your `ProviderProfile` object is created on-chain, and you will be redirected to your new **Provider Dashboard**, where you can start creating and listing experiences.
