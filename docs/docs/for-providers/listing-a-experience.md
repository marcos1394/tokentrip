# Creating & Listing Your Experiences

As a provider, you have full control over your inventory. You can mint new `ExperienceNFT`s and choose how to sell them.

### From Your Dashboard

Navigate to your **Provider Dashboard**. You will see two main sections: "Active Listings" and "Inventory (Ready to List)".

#### Minting a New Experience (Admin Action)

Currently, the creation of new base `ExperienceNFT` types is handled by the TokenTrip admin team to ensure quality and consistency. You would work with us to define your experience, and we would mint the NFT to your wallet. You then have full ownership to manage and sell it.

#### Listing an Experience

From your "Inventory" tab, find the NFT you wish to sell and click on the "Actions" menu. You have two primary options:

1.  **List for Sale**: This creates a fixed-price listing on the marketplace. You can choose to set the price in either **SUI** or **TKT**.
2.  **Start Auction**: This lists the experience in the Auction House. You will set a starting price (in SUI) and a duration for the auction.

Once an item is listed, it will move from your "Inventory" to your "Active Listings" tab.
