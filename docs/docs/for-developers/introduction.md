# Building on TokenTrip

Welcome, developer! We're excited that you're interested in building on top of the TokenTrip ecosystem.

Our protocol is designed to be open and composable. We believe that the future of travel and experiences will be built by a wide range of creators and developers, and we aim to provide the core infrastructure to make that possible.

### What You Can Build

* **Custom Storefronts**: Build unique, niche marketplace frontends that plug into our backend smart contracts.
* **Third-Party Integrations**: Integrate TokenTrip's functionality into other dApps.
* **New Experience Types**: Propose new standards and features for on-chain experiences.
* **Tooling & Analytics**: Create tools for providers or analytics dashboards for the DAO.

This section will provide an overview of our smart contract architecture. For hands-on development, please visit our official **[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/your-username/tokentrip)**.
