# Guide for Collectors: Joining Exclusive Channels

Owning an `ExperienceNFT` on TokenTrip is more than just holding a digital asset—it's your key to exclusive communities. Here’s how to unlock your access.

### Step 1: Join the TokenTrip Discord
If you haven't already, join our official community server using the link on our website.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Verification Channel
Once inside, find the channel named **`#✅｜verify-wallet`**. This is where our community bot lives.

### Step 3: Connect Your Wallet
1.  In the `#✅｜verify-wallet` channel, you will see a message from the Guild.xyz bot with a button that says **"Join Guild to get roles"**. Click it.
2.  You will be taken to the TokenTrip page on Guild.xyz.
3.  Click the "Connect Wallet" button and select the Sui wallet that holds your TokenTrip NFTs. You will be asked to sign a message to prove you own the wallet (this is a gasless transaction).

### Step 4: Get Your Roles!
Guild will automatically scan your wallet for eligible `ExperienceNFTs`. For every NFT you own that has a corresponding gated community, the bot will instantly grant you the required role in our Discord server.

### Step 5: Enjoy Your Exclusive Access
Once you have the role, the private channel(s) associated with your NFTs will automatically become visible to you in the Discord channel list. You can now chat with the provider and other collectors who share your experience!
