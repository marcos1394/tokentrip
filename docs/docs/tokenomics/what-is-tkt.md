# The TokenTrip Token (TKT)

**TKT** is the native utility and governance token of the TokenTrip ecosystem. It is the engine that drives our user-owned economy, designed to align incentives and reward active participants.

With a **fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens**, TKT is designed to be a sustainable asset that grows in value as the platform succeeds.

The token serves three primary functions:
1.  **Utility**: Unlocks benefits and is used as a medium of exchange within the platform.
2.  **Governance**: Grants the community control over the future of the protocol.
3.  **Value Accrual**: Captures the economic success of the marketplace and distributes it back to stakeholders.
