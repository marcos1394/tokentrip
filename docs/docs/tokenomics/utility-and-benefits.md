# TKT Utility & Benefits

TKT is deeply integrated into the TokenTrip platform, providing tangible value to its holders.

### For All Users

* **Medium of Exchange**: Use TKT as a native currency to buy and sell experiences on the marketplace.
* **Staking for Benefits**: By staking TKT, users can receive significant discounts on platform transaction fees. We use a tiered system: the more you stake, the more you save.

### For Providers

* **Quality Bond**: Providers can stake TKT to receive a "Verified Provider" badge on their profile. This acts as a bond of quality and trust, making their listings more attractive to buyers. This stake can be "slashed" (penalized) by the DAO in clear cases of fraud, protecting the community.

### For DAO Participants

* **Governance Rights**: Staked TKT grants voting power in the TokenTrip DAO. Participants can vote on key decisions, such as changes to the fee structure or the allocation of treasury funds.
* **Right to Propose**: Holding a minimum amount of TKT is required to create a new governance proposal, ensuring that those who propose changes have a vested interest in the platform's success.
