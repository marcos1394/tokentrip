# Token Distribution & Supply

To foster a decentralized and community-first ecosystem, the 1 billion TKT tokens are allocated as follows.

### Allocation Breakdown

| Category                          | Percentage | Amount of Tokens | Purpose                                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Community Treasury & Ecosystem**| 50%        | 500,000,000      | The largest fund, governed by the DAO to finance grants, marketing, and liquidity. |
| **Core Team & Advisors** | 20%        | 200,000,000      | Reward for the core team, with long-term vesting to ensure commitment.             |
| **Seed Investors** | 15%        | 150,000,000      | For early backers who provided initial capital.                                    |
| **Public Sale (LBP)** | 10%        | 100,000,000      | For fair price discovery and initial distribution via a Liquidity Bootstrapping Pool.|
| **Strategic Airdrop** | 5%         | 50,000,000       | To acquire key initial users and providers.                                        |

### Vesting Schedule

To ensure long-term alignment, tokens allocated to the team and investors are subject to a vesting schedule.

| Group              | Cliff Period (Full Lock) | Vesting Period (Linear)   | Total Duration |
| ------------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------- | -------------- |
| **Team & Advisors**| 12 months                | 36 months (after cliff)   | **4 years** |
| **Investors** | 6 months                 | 24 months (after cliff)   | **2.5 years** |
