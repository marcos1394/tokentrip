# Welcome to TokenTrip

**TokenTrip is a decentralized travel and experience marketplace built on the Sui blockchain.**

Our mission is to build a fairer, more transparent, and user-owned economy for travel and entertainment. We transform real-world experiences—from concert tickets to hotel stays and guided tours—into unique digital assets (NFTs) that you truly own.

### What does this mean for you?

-   **For Travelers:** Your ticket is no longer just a QR code; it's a liquid asset. Can't make it to an event? Sell your ticket on our open secondary market. Want to co-own a luxury villa with friends? Fractionalize the ownership. With TokenTrip, you are in control.
-   **For Providers:** Escape the high fees and centralized control of traditional platforms. On TokenTrip, you connect directly with your customers, set your own terms, and earn fair royalties on every resale of your experiences.

This documentation will guide you through every aspect of the TokenTrip ecosystem, from buying your first experience to participating in governance that shapes the future of the platform.
