# Simple Sign-On with zkLogin

Welcome to the easiest way to enter Web3. TokenTrip uses **zkLogin**, a revolutionary feature from Sui that lets you create a wallet and sign in using the social accounts you already know and trust.

**No seed phrases, no complicated setup.**



### How It Works

It's as simple as logging into any other modern web application.

1.  **Click "Sign In"**: On the TokenTrip homepage, click the main sign-in or connect button.
2.  **Choose Your Provider**: Select your preferred social account, such as Google, Twitch, or Facebook.
3.  **Authorize**: A familiar pop-up from your chosen provider will ask for your permission to connect. Approve it.

That's it! In the background, a secure Sui wallet address is instantly created and linked to your social account. You can now start exploring, buying, and interacting with the marketplace. Your digital assets will be automatically secured and tied to your login.

### For Advanced Users: Traditional Wallets

If you're a crypto veteran and prefer to manage your own keys and recovery phrases, you can still connect using a traditional Sui browser wallet like **Suiet**, **Sui Wallet**, or **Glass Wallet**. Simply choose the "Connect Wallet" option instead of a social login.
