# The TokenTrip DAO

TokenTrip is not a traditional company; it is a **Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO)**. This means the platform is collectively owned and governed by its community of TKT token holders.

Our mission is to create a self-sustaining protocol that operates transparently and fairly, with its future direction decided by the very people who use it and give it value.
