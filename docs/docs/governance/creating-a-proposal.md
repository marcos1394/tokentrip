# How to Create a Proposal

Any community member with sufficient TKT can create a proposal to influence the future of TokenTrip.

### Proposal Requirement

To prevent spam and ensure proposals are submitted by committed members, there is a minimum requirement to create a proposal:

-   **You must hold at least 10,000 TKT in your wallet.**

These tokens are only used to verify your eligibility and **are not spent** or locked when creating a proposal.

### Proposal Types

Our DAO supports several types of on-chain actions:

1.  **Transfer TKT**: Propose a transfer of TKT funds from the DAO Treasury. This is used for funding marketing campaigns, developer grants, paying contractors, or other initiatives.
2.  **Signal**: A proposal with no direct on-chain action. This is used to signal community consensus on off-chain matters, such as approving a legal agreement, a strategic partnership, or a general statement of intent.
3.  **Update DAO Parameter**: Propose a change to the DAO's own rules. This is how the DAO governs itself. You can propose changes to:
    * Minimum TKT to propose
    * Voting period duration
    * Quorum percentage
    * Approval threshold

To create a proposal, navigate to the **[Create Proposal page](/governance/create)** on our platform.
